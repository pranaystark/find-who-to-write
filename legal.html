Legal and privacy

This is not legal advice. It states how this civic directory is meant to operate — including on GitHub Pages — so you know what is collected, what is not, and who is responsible when you press send.

How to read this

Australian law is what applies to the operator. Hosting on GitHub Pages means GitHub (a US company) also processes connection data under its own terms. This page describes the operator’s position. It does not bind GitHub, Parliament, the AEC, or Home Affairs.

What this site is

A static, independent civic directory: public federal contacts by postcode or suburb, plus optional letter drafts. You copy text and send it yourself. Snapshot of the 48th Parliament (APH lists, September 2026).

It is not: a government website; a political party; a law practice; a registered migration agent; a health service; or a way to lodge, grant, refuse, or accelerate a visa.

Migration Act — not immigration assistance

Under the Migration Act 1958, “immigration assistance” includes advising on visas, preparing applications or statements for the Department, and representing someone to Home Affairs — generally only a registered MARA agent or a lawyer in the usual course of legal practice may do that for others.

This site does none of that. It does not:

  • prepare or lodge a visa application;
  • tell you which visa to apply for, or whether you will be granted one;
  • fill ImmiAccount fields;
  • write to Home Affairs, APH, or an MP for you;
  • store your Home Affairs reference.

Drafts are general civic wording about publicly known processing settings (including Direction 117) and optional facts you tick. They are not advice on your eligibility. If you need visa strategy, use a registered migration agent or a lawyer. If you run a fork that emails MPs for other people, or charges to “handle the letter,” you may cross into assistance or a paid service — don’t.

Who is the author of the email

You are. The browser never submits the draft to our host. Copy/paste is your publication. You are responsible for truth, tone, attachments, and volume. Do not mass-mail senators. Do not attach passports, health records, or an ImmiAccount export unless an electorate office asks. Defamation, harassment, and spam rules apply to your send, not to a button on this page.

Privacy — operator

The operator does not run accounts, does not use analytics cookies, and does not receive your name, suburb search, lodgement date, Home Affairs number, or tick-box answers. Those values stay in your browser until you copy them or close the tab.

The Privacy Act 1988 (APPs) applies to agencies and to many organisations (for example larger businesses, or anyone trading in personal information). A small static volunteer directory that does not collect personal information is not trying to be a health file or a mailing list. If a fork adds forms, analytics, or chat, that fork may start collecting personal information and should get its own advice.

Health-related ticks (“strain on wellbeing”) are optional wording for your letter. Do not send clinical detail unless you choose to. This site is not a health service.

Privacy — GitHub Pages as host

If this site is served from *.github.io (or a custom domain pointed at GitHub Pages), your browser requests files from GitHub. GitHub may log IP address, URL, user agent, and similar connection metadata under the GitHub privacy statement. The operator does not get a copy of those logs in the ordinary Pages setup. Data may be processed outside Australia. We do not ask GitHub to combine that with visa files — we never have the visa files.

Third-party links (APH, AEC, Home Affairs, AHRC, Ombudsman, newsrooms, Lifeline) are their sites and their privacy policies.

Human rights language

Australia is a party to treaties that speak to family life. That is not the same as a court or the AHRC having found a violation in your case. Drafts on this site are written to say the delay engages family life and to ask for an enquiry — not to declare a violation. An AHRC or Ombudsman complaint is a record you file yourself; it is not a queue jump.

Accuracy and no warranty

Contacts come from Parliament address CSVs. Maps are AEC-derived and can lag redistributions. Postcodes often span more than one seat — confirm on the AEC. Ministers, shadows, and committee seats change. The site is provided as-is. The operator is not liable for writing the wrong MP, a stale phone number, or a Department outcome. Australian Consumer Law still prohibits misleading claims; this site does not claim to speed visas.

Copyright and data

  • Names, electorates, official phones, electorate offices: taken from publicly issued Parliament of Australia address lists. We use official contacts only. Verify on aph.gov.au.
  • Suburb / postcode / division: derived from AEC-related open data. Boundaries remain the AEC’s. Not an official AEC product.
  • Drafts and this site’s wording: the operator’s; edit them into your own letter.

Non-commercial. Do not sell the list, scrape it into a marketing database, or wrap it in a paid “we will contact Parliament for you” product.

Spam, elections, impersonation

  • Spam Act 2003: this host does not send you or MPs electronic messages. Your own email to an MP is your communication with an elected office.
  • Electoral advertising: a volunteer directory is not, by itself, an electoral ad. Paid promotion intended to influence votes may need a Commonwealth authorisation statement. Get advice before ads.
  • Do not use the Commonwealth Coat of Arms or mimic gov.au. This site is independent.

Security on GitHub Pages

GitHub Pages serves static files over HTTPS. It does not apply the extra HTTP headers in _headers / Netlify config (those are for Cloudflare Pages or Netlify). This site therefore also sets a Content-Security-Policy and referrer policy in HTML. That is weaker than server headers but is what Pages allows. Do not add third-party scripts, trackers, or a backend. Refresh APH data after by-elections.

Children

The directory is aimed at adult sponsors writing representatives. It is not directed at children.
